## Status definitions

- `ACTIVE`: has a live owner and a currently running process or explicitly
  admitted current task. Do not duplicate it.
- `BLOCKED`: a concrete dependency prevents the next transition. Preserve the
  evidence and do not substitute a source, cohort, credential, or protocol.
- `PARKED`: intentionally dormant. It may be useful, but there is no current
  execution authority or owner.
- `COMPLETE`: the declared transition has terminal evidence and no work remains
  within that scope. A new hypothesis needs new authority.
- `SUPERSEDED`: a newer implementation, protocol, or owner replaces this lane.
  Do not merge or reactivate the predecessor.
- `ORPHANED`: useful-looking code or evidence exists without a current owner or
  trustworthy closeout. Preserve it and review before adoption; never infer
  authority from its presence.
