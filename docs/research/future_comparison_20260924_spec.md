## Inputs and frozen methods

All four forecasts use the **same complete active runner field and same retained,
verified Sportsbet fixed WIN receipt** selected by the existing prediction path.
The market vector is normalized inverse recorded decimal odds. It is a
probability comparator, not an executable price or estimated fair-price truth.
The production predictor/model/configuration are unchanged. Both experimental
methods use their separately fitted artifacts in
`artifacts/research_comparison/frozen_20260924/`, with exact registry SHA binding.

The two experimental recipes are #193's capped L2=1 conditional residual:
16 form features plus literal box at strength 1, and the original 16 features at
strength 0.5. Both were fitted at full residual strength; half strength scales
the fitted residual at prediction, exactly as shortlisted. No future fitting,
feature changes, calibration or threshold selection is permitted. Production
uses its retained merged DB/card features. Candidates use the canonical raw-card
history route used in #193, capped at 20 accepted prior starts. These are
explicitly different feature constructions from the same source card, not a
claim that reusing production feature rows reproduces the historical recipe.

Use the first consumed eligible existing prediction attempt for each exact race.
Receipt capture/append time must be **T−10 through T−2 inclusive**; admission,
candidate completion and whole bundle publication must be **strictly before
T−2**. The receipt must already exist at admission. Thus this asks about the first
available qualified retained quote in that window, not the closing market or a
latest-price search. No later receipt, model, race, retry or field replacement
can rescue a consumed comparison. A T−2 collector capture which completes too
late is missing evidence, not a relaxed cutoff. All existing source, freshness,
runner, grade, retention, model and bundle guards still apply.

Histories must precede the target date; same-date and future histories reject.
Source capture times and byte hashes remain in the retained bundle. A byte-level
CSV projection checks history dates before decoding result-valued card fields.
A metadata-only retained DB date query excludes protected history from the
paired record. Conservatively reject any shared history dated July 15–October

The approval must separately allow fixed, machine-only historical feature

No other protected cohort's outcomes become usable merely because they are past.

## Admission, sealing and denominators

The existing supervisor records immutable first observations from its verified
current index, before capture/retention success. Late first observations remain
visible and are marked as late. Index-verification failures are preserved in the
operational package. This is an **observed-index population**, not proof of a
complete bookmaker/venue schedule. Disclose time without an active collector,
missing index intervals and venues never observed from existing campaign logs.
There is no new discovery source, collector, scheduler or provider request.

The worker consumes a comparison race claim before preflight. Preflight failures,
crashes with only a dispatch, missing inputs, model failures, late forecasts and
publication failures remain missing predictions. Admission precedes scoring;
each record binds job/race/runner identity, plan, admission, model, config and
shared input hashes. Completion binds the already published bundle manifest and
actual publication timestamp. Existing bundle verification authenticates the
comparison files; replay independently reconstructs both candidate features and
all four probabilities. A candidate failure does not overwrite production or
another candidate. Previously sealed files cannot be replaced.

At the endpoint report: observed races/dates/venues, first observed too late,
worker dispatches, admissions, failures by stage/model, no-attempt observations,
all-four seals, official result closure and final paired races. Retain individual
failure records, not just totals. Main collector claim/retention/terminal ledgers
supply distinctions between no capture, failed capture, failed retention and
no dispatch. The primary comparison uses the **same all-four valid races**;
no pair-specific cherry-picking. Separately report success for each model and
coverage by date/venue. Do not claim silent model failure is an abstention rule.

The immutable one-shot evaluator claim and membership file are written before
any official-result reads. Only admitted exact race IDs are requested from the
existing read-only official-result adapter. Full field identity, source,
timestamps and unambiguous finish positions must validate. Scratches/changed
fields, cancellations, dead heats and missing/ambiguous evidence are preserved
as result-closure failures. They cannot be repaired by swapping forecasts or
changing membership. With any unresolved common seal, scores on complete
results are descriptive only and no confirmatory advantage may be declared.
No complete-result subset is silently presented as the whole population.

## Evaluation fixed before outcomes

Race-weighted log loss and multiclass Brier sum are the paired primary measures.
Use each frozen candidate versus market and versus production: **four fixed
contrasts**. Resample complete Melbourne calendar dates, 20,000 replicates,
seed 20260924. Report 98.75% two-sided percentile intervals per contrast
(Bonferroni family alpha 0.05 across four contrasts). Within a contrast, require
both log loss and Brier upper bounds below zero before claiming improvement;
this is an intersection rule, not an assumption that the losses are independent.
Advancing a candidate requires improvement against both comparators, adequate
coverage, complete closure, and assessment of period stability. These intervals
are approximate, not a guarantee against shared-dog dependence across dates.

Report fixed ten-bin runner calibration with counts and predicted/observed
frequencies; no calibration fitting. Descriptive top-choice accuracy, winner
rank and reciprocal rank use box order to break probability ties. Report every
calendar month and venue, leave-one-date-out log-loss differences, date counts,
field sizes and coverage. No best-month/subgroup selection and no staking,
returns or betting claims. No new favourite/outsider rule is selected here.

[Precision planning](future_comparison_evidence/precision_planning.json) uses
only the old 177 inspected races over 15 dates. Date-cluster standard errors of
candidate-minus-market log loss were 0.007972 (box) and 0.004185 (half).
For half-width 0.01 nats, four-contrast normal quantile 2.497705, and an explicit
factor-two inflation of variance, the estimate is
`ceil(15 * (2.497705 * SE / 0.01)^2 * 2)`: **119 and 33 scoreable dates**.
Use the larger target. At the old 11.8 races/date, 119 dates would mean about
**1,405 races**, not an inherited 1,000-race stopping rule. A fixed 168-day window
allows 49 non-scoreable dates; it does not promise that retention/history gates
will achieve this. Only 15 old dates inform the estimate; production-relative
variance, future dependence and collection yield remain unknown. A tiny gain
may remain unresolved even at the target. Report achieved precision and
shortfall at the fixed endpoint, with no outcome-driven extension.
