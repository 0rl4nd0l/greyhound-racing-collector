## Windows, identities, truncation and deduplication

For research/candidates, `H` is the canonical accepted history, newest first, capped at **20 retained starts**. `R3=H[:3]`, `R5=H[:5]`; these are starts, not calendar windows. Invalid dates and target/post-target dates are rejected. Deduplication compares normalized date, venue, distance, grade, finish, box, margin and explicit ordering. Multiple starts on one day require a consistent explicit race ordinal/timestamp; ambiguous ordering fails. It is not deduplication by stable provider event ID. Each accepted start counts even if its finish or context is missing. [Canonical parser](../../scripts/build_form_only_v1_packet.py#L490-L563).

Production history is oldest first, without that cap; recent subsets are the last three/five merged rows. Merge identity is date, uppercased venue, numeric distance, normalized grade, time and finish. DB rows are encountered first and retained on equal keys. Disagreement in time/finish/venue spelling can leave two records of the same actual start; no empirical incidence is established here. The apparent replace-missing-time branch cannot reconcile a different time because time is itself part of the key. Ordering falls back to date/race number/race ID/venue rather than the canonical parser's explicit same-day proof. [Merge](../../scripts/run_shadow_non_tgr_rf_evaluation.py#L1851-L1880), [feature windows](../../scripts/run_feature_recovery_execution_v1.py#L1491-L1506). This is a demonstrated implementation limitation, not a quantified production defect on the available development cohort.

## Compact inventory

`F(S)` means known numeric finishes among starts in `S`; `W(S)` counts finishes equal to 1; `P(S)` counts finishes ≤3. These place rates use top three regardless of field size or paid-place terms. In research/candidates, an empty finish denominator gives null, averages ignore missing values, and feature-row aggregates round to eight decimal places. Production's `rate` divides by **all rows** in the subset and gives **0 for an empty subset**; unknown finishes count as non-wins/non-places. Thus a production zero rate is ambiguous between no rows and rows with no qualifying wins, including unknown finishes. Counts and observed zero margins are numeric and receive no automatic missing flag. [Canonical aggregates](../../scripts/build_form_only_v1_packet.py#L566-L628), [research aliases](../../scripts/offline_form_packet.py#L29-L44), [production formulas](../../scripts/run_feature_recovery_execution_v1.py#L1478-L1522).

|---|---|---|

## Interpretation constraints with practical consequences

**Recent equals career because history is short.** Equality is not hardcoded: canonical formulas use different slices, but they are the same set whenever accepted history has at most five starts. Parent audit of the qualified 331-race/2,360-runner development cards found accepted lengths `{1:31, 2:43, 3:25, 4:42, 5:2219}`, zero accepted-history rejections and zero missing/invalid finishes. The 20-start implementation cap did not cause this five-start ceiling. It is the retained source surface. The prior 1,251 evaluated-runner equality is therefore explained without claiming whole-career completion or a copied-column bug. These counts are cross-referenced to the accompanying generated audit, not recomputed by this code-only inventory.

Duplicated standardized columns contribute through `x*(beta_recent+beta_career)`. Under a symmetric ridge objective, for a fixed combined coefficient `b`, splitting equally minimizes the penalty: `beta1²+beta2² = b²/2`, versus `b²` for one column. Duplication therefore changes effective regularization along that direction; it neither supplies independent information nor mechanically doubles predictions. Imputation, scaling, race centering, the tanh cap and softmax also intervene. Dropping one column without adjusting/refitting is a model-method change, outside the frozen route. The development naming/provenance correction preserves both columns. Source objective: [linear fit](../../scripts/offline_systematic_search.py#L127-L143).

**Grade and venue context are not universal sporting measurements.** Canonical grade normalization removes `TIER 3`/`BOTTOM UP` prefixes and `HEAT`/`FINAL` suffixes, maps documented aliases and grade spellings, and preserves other strings as tokens. No jurisdiction is part of the equality key. Normalizing labels cannot establish equivalent competition strength across states. Production has a separate grade alias map; unmatched labels may remain literal. Canonical venue aliases pool Q1/Q2/Q-straight as QOT, Richmond straight with Richmond, and Murray Bridge straight with Murray Bridge. Same-venue count thus does not necessarily mean same track layout; same-distance does not require same venue. Sources: [canonical grade and venue](../../scripts/build_form_only_v1_packet.py#L340-L369), [production normalization](../../scripts/run_feature_recovery_execution_v1.py#L479-L493), [existing layout evidence](offline_systematic_foundation_audit.md#feature-measurement-limitations).

**Margin is source-recorded, not demonstrated beaten distance.** Canonical parsing passes numeric `MGN` through unchanged, including winners; the production card parser labels the same source field `beaten_margin` without converting its meaning. DB parsing uses `beaten_margin or margin`, meaning numeric zero can fall through to the other column. Neither code path documents source units, sign, winner-margin reference or consistency across jurisdictions. Parent audit's positive winner margins make a universal “distance beaten” interpretation particularly unsafe. Retain the value as recorded, identify its source, and separate winner/nonwinner coverage for diagnosis. Do not invert, zero or re-sign winner margins without a verified source contract. Sources: [canonical parse](../../scripts/build_form_only_v1_packet.py#L518-L526), [card parse](../../scripts/run_shadow_non_tgr_rf_evaluation.py#L1826-L1836), [DB fallback](../../scripts/run_feature_recovery_execution_v1.py#L737-L743).

**Missing context is often genuinely unobserved.** The qualified card audit reports missing venue/distance/grade win-rate inputs for 940/523/746 runners respectively, while all corresponding source contexts and finishes are known: these are no matching retained starts, not failed parsing. Longer independently qualified histories could resolve some; new code cannot invent them. The unknown-target count defect above is real but affects zero feature values in this population. Future incomplete histories still require per-feature known-value denominators; `starts_same_*` alone is not a valid rate denominator when finishes are missing. Hash integrity and complete current rosters do not prove historical completeness.

## Preprocessing and scoring

All 16 base values are followed by 16 missing indicators. Training medians fill nonfinite/missing values; a training column with no finite values uses an imputation median of zero. This imputation fallback does not reinterpret the original value as observed zero: the missing indicator remains set. Training means/stds standardize **both** values and indicators; near-zero training std becomes 1. At prediction time, each standardized column is centered over the actual race field. The model then computes `z=X beta`, capped residual `r=0.35*tanh(z/0.35)`, and `p=softmax(log(normalized_inverse_WIN_odds)+strength*r)`. Full and half strengths apply after the cap. Sources: [research preprocessor and objective](../../scripts/offline_systematic_search.py#L112-L143), [frozen production scorer](../../src/predictor/market_form_residual.py#L753-L804), [candidate scorer](https://github.com/0rl4nd0l/greyhound-racing-collector/blob/1cd7ccff79b0fed33e5411b9bf1fb3f24b5ac280/src/predictor/comparison_candidates.py#L101-L126).

Consequently a runner with no missing raw inputs can receive missingness contributions relative to rivals. If every runner shares an indicator value, that column contributes zero after race centering. A numeric count of zero never activates its missing indicator. This distinguishes observed absence of matching retained starts from unavailable target context, and explains why changing a count from zero to null can affect every runner's relative transform. Existing #197 decomposition found nonzero missing-indicator contributions for 1,033 evaluated runners and 159/177 races with at least one missing base input; these are retained descriptive results, not a new causal or prospective finding. [Prior explanation](market_explanation_20260929_results.md#what-mechanically-changed-the-probabilities).

The manually embedded-form research adapter is a fourth interface, not the frozen card-only candidate route. It uses ascending explicit event timestamps, supplied prior race IDs, validated finishes 1–10, literal normalized venue/grade, ±50m distance, and empty-subset rates of zero. It has no DB acquisition and does not certify complete career history. Do not use it as evidence of canonical candidate parity. [Adapter](../../src/predictor/manual_research_scoring.py#L293-L429).
