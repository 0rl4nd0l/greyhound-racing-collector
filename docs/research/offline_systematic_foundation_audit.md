## Feature measurement limitations

The canonical parser collapses `LADBROKES-Q-STRAIGHT`, Q1 Lakeside and Q2 Parklands (and Q1L/QST) into `QOT`; Richmond straight into `RICH`; Murray Bridge straight into `MURR`. Its `same_venue` aggregates therefore pool layouts. Legacy 16-feature compatibility should disclose this measurement limitation. It is **not** temporal leakage, but a time/sectional extension must not claim exact track comparability from that alias alone. Conservatively mark ambiguous-layout comparisons unavailable or use source-defined exact layout evidence. Same-distance in the legacy method means distance only, across tracks; same-grade means exact normalized category, without a defensible universal class ordering.

[The previous coverage audit](offline_20260924_model_provenance.md) already showed single-digit PIR equal to finishing position in all 6,920 such observations; it cannot be relabelled as early speed. Sectional measurement units/call definitions, beaten versus winner time semantics, grade equivalence across jurisdictions and sample-size uncertainty remain limitations for additional features. Prior elapsed-time values can be compared conservatively within demonstrated track/distance contexts, but they do not automatically establish intrinsic speed or a market error.
