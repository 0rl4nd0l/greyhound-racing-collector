The result controller checks scheduling metadata every ten minutes. It may
pause only the exact pinned collector with fresh healthy idle state, a complete
current-date census, no unknown jump times and over 70 minutes to the next
jump. It checks retained child lifetimes before stopping and after native
drain. Source and campaign locks remain mandatory. A batch lasts at most five
minutes, each cycle at most 60 seconds and its fetch at most 25 seconds. It
rechecks remaining lead-in headroom before each cycle. Result processing never
interrupts an active collection lead-in. No suitable gap means waiting, then
an explicit missing-result disposition if closure cannot finish in time.

Durable intent precedes stopping. Normal failure runs restoration in `finally`;
`ExecStopPost --recover-only` and subsequent timer invocations recover a prior
intent without repeating its charged request. A source denial or unknown child
ownership prevents unsafe restoration. Successful restoration requires fresh
post-start health, rather than merely a nonzero process ID.
