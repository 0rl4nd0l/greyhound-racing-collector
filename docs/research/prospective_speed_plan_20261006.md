## Precision and interpretation

The six already inspected legacy evaluation dates have standard deviation **0.0162083** in their date-mean speed-minus-baseline log-loss differences. A rough equal-date normal approximation needs about **180 dates** for a 95% half-width equal to the exploratory 0.0023721 gain, or **367 dates** for 80% power at two-sided 5%. These are planning diagnostics, not promised sample requirements: six exposed dates, date dependence, unequal daily race counts and prospective coverage changes make the estimates unstable.

Only two fresh dates are available under the existing allocation. Their normal-approximation half-width is approximately 0.02246, shown only as a scale comparison, not a valid two-date confidence interval. The planned analysis therefore reports date-level changes and influence, with **no date-cluster interval** and no claim of precise confirmation. A larger future population would need its own allocation decision; this plan does not extend itself because results look encouraging.
