## Estimands, uncertainty and missingness

Primary complete-result summaries: race-weighted log loss and multiclass Brier
sum on identical all-four races. Fractional outcomes for dead heats; descriptive
ranking gives winner-share credit, and probability ties use box order. Report
fixed ten-bin calibration, all months/venues and leave-one-date-out differences.
No returns, staking, best subgroup selection or new favourite/outsider rule.

Four prespecified contrasts: each experimental candidate minus market and minus
production. Both loss measures must improve for a contrast; both comparators
must improve to advance a candidate.20,000 date-block resamples, seed 20260924,
98.75% two-sided percentile intervals for each contrast (Bonferroni across 4;
intersection rule for both losses). Also resample entire Monday-based weeks and
require the conclusion to survive this sensitivity. These approximate intervals
assume sufficiently weak between-block dependence; repeated dogs/seasonality
can defeat that assumption. They are not a guarantee of coverage.

Under 40 contributing dates or 12 weeks, output is descriptive and cannot report
an inferential pass. These are conservative minimum block counts for this
proposal, not a theorem that 40 dates/12 weeks ensure validity. Bootstrap replicate
count does not create independent observations. Inspect period stability and
coverage without picking favourable periods.

Complete-case findings with unresolved results are descriptive. The executable
sensitivity analysis does **not** require perfect completion: for each unresolved
race use all possible winner identities (including fractional dead-heat mixtures)
and possible void contribution 0. Candidate-minus-reference log-loss differences
are `log(p_reference / p_candidate)` for each possible winner; Brier differences
are `sum(p_candidate²-p_reference²)-2*(p_candidate-p_reference)`.
Aggregate the minimum/maximum possible differences over **all common seals**,
with outer date/week bootstrap intervals. Known complete results contribute
exact differences. The all-seal denominator is an intention-to-forecast paired
contrast; it differs in scale from loss averaged over known valid results.

A conditional robust-improvement statement needs both worst-case upper bounds
below 0, for both comparators, adequate block counts, and stable coverage.
It assumes unknown outcomes belong to the sealed field or the race is void.
Actual outside-field or unresolved identity/provenance rejections block a broader
claim: those probabilities do not define that event. An unconditional claim for
all common seals additionally needs source-backed field/void certification for
unresolved cases; missing results alone cannot supply it. No result bounds can
repair missing predictions or establish representativeness of all opportunities.
Thus imperfect closure can still be informative, without asserting that missing
cases resemble observed ones or quietly excluding them.

No interim metrics or performance-driven changes. Outcome-blind uptime, timing,
field alignment, retention counts and closure-status monitoring is permitted.
At 112 days stop admission regardless of sample size; close after 14 days, evaluate
once. Operational outages reduce evidence rather than move the endpoint. A new
study requires a new decision, not continuation until significance.

## Realistic precision and stopping budget

Only 15 old inspected development dates inform the variance estimate; no new
search was run. Box-minus-market old date-cluster SE 0.007972 is uncertain, and
production-relative variance is unknown. The previous 119-date calculation
assumed historical density 11.8 races/date and doubled variance. A 168-day window
never demonstrated 119 usable dates.

[Recorded scenarios](future_comparison_20260928_evidence/precision_scenarios.json)
assume 8 races/completed 90-minute session, informed by one live session, not a
measured daily capacity. The central 16-week scenario has 5 sessions/week,
80% availability,90% common seals and 95% closure: about 64 contributing dates and
438 closed races. Allowing lower race density and doubled variance gives an
approximate simultaneous box-versus-market half-width **0.0179 nats**, and a
marginal 80%-power effect scale **0.0239 nats**. With variance multipliers 1/2/4,
central half-widths are approximately 0.0127/0.0179/0.0253. These are planning
assumptions, not measured future power or joint power for both losses/comparators.
A gain near 0.005–0.01 may remain unresolved; four months or six months cannot be
promised to prove an edge. The finite 16-week/120-hour allocation is a first
prospective information budget, not a target selected to guarantee significance.
