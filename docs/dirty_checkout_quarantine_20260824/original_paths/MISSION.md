# Mission: Understand the Greyhound Prediction System

## Why
Build a reliable mental model of how race predictions are produced so research, runtime, and promotion decisions are based on what the system really uses—not on a promising experiment or an ambiguous artifact.

## Success looks like
- Explain the live shadow prediction path from source capture to ranked runners.
- Distinguish deployed inputs from report-only research inputs.
- Identify which evidence would justify changing the predictor.

## Constraints
- Ground explanations in repository code, sealed artifacts, and verified runtime configuration.
- Keep provenance, pre-jump timing, identity, and research-only boundaries explicit.

## Out of scope
- Betting recommendations, staking, or claims of profitable edge.
- Activating, retraining, or promoting a model during teaching sessions.
